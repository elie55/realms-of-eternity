# Realms of Eternity — Audio Credits

## Music, sound effects and voices

- All music, sound effects and voices in this version (v14) were made for Realms of Eternity with ElevenLabs (Creator plan, commercial licence for generated output): Sound Generation for the sound effects, Eleven Music for the soundtrack, Voice Design and Text-to-Speech for every character, rival and creature voice.
- Designed transient and sub-bass layers were synthesised in code for the project.
- The creature voices (summons, attacks and deaths) use designed voices; animal and construct cards use real animal sounds generated the same way.

## Attribution
